## Written read

## What separated winners from losers

Across the three rebuilds, picks beat the shortlist median only 38%, 67%, and 36% of the time, and picks beat the passed-over group in only one of three rebuilds (+4.59% vs +1.70%). In the other two, picks trailed or barely matched passed-over names (-1.69% vs -0.92%; +2.20% vs +1.30%). The biggest single-name moves were almost all misses: the five best movers in each rebuild were nearly always in the "passed over" bucket (QNST +14.8%, MPC +21.9%, SIG +17.4%, etc.), while the worst movers were also concentrated there, except for one picked name (WMG +13.6%). This looks like a sample where the shortlist and the passed-over pool are hard to tell apart on realized return, not a sign the process is adding value.

## Where the judges' reasoning held up or missed

IESC was marked "slightly more likely than not to lag" in all three rebuilds and then fell -21.8%, -22.3%, and -25.8% — consistent with the bearish lean, though the edge_pts given were small or even positive (+1.3 to +2.7), so the direction was right but the sizing looks off. Elsewhere the judges' narrative calls didn't track outcomes well: STX and DELL were called "draw"/"bull" on AI themes and both dropped double digits (-30.7%, -16.9%); ABNB, BKH, EXPE were "draw" calls and rose +9–11%. The "credit spreads" theme shows up on both big winners (ABNB, HRB) and big losers (FORM), so that single factor doesn't discriminate outcomes in this sample.

## Hypotheses to test (each with how we would test it)

- H1: The quality (q), value (v), and low-vol/liquidity (l) signals carry more signal than panel_p or composite. Test: track q/v/l rank correlation with forward returns over the next 10+ rebuilds; see if it stays above the ~±0.22 luck band.
- H2: News_score and momentum (m) are mildly anti-correlated with returns (m: -0.20, -0.11, -0.25 across rebuilds). Test: log m and news_score separately from composite and check sign stability over more cycles before treating as informative.
- H3: IESC-style "modest edge despite bearish thesis" picks systematically underperform. Test: flag all cases where edge_pts is positive but thesis sentiment is bear/draw-bear, and track their forward returns separately.

## What not to conclude yet

With only 9–14 trading days and ~80 names per rebuild, individual correlations like q (+0.28), v (+0.25), l (+0.28) are close to the ±0.22 band attributable to luck alone — call these hypotheses, not findings. The pick-vs-shortlist-vs-passed-over comparisons also come from just three rebuilds; one rebuild (+4.59% picks) look good, two don't, which is too small a sample to judge the selection process itself. Do not change live settings based on this; only log and keep tracking the signals above.
